Reading with intention.

Approve this sample site in the extension, enable viewing in the dashboard, and leave each paragraph visible for several seconds. No sample data is added automatically.

Observe a section

A reading companion can combine several signals to estimate exposure to a paragraph. The section must be visible, the browser should have focus, and the reader should spend sufficient time near the material. These observations estimate attention; they do not establish comprehension.

Make a prediction

Scrolling quickly through a document provides little evidence that every sentence was read. Pausing and returning to a passage provide stronger evidence of exposure. A useful system keeps these observations separate from the results of later practice questions.

Check your understanding

Practice questions encourage a learner to retrieve information without immediately looking at the source. Explanations and source references make it easier to review mistakes. A trustworthy learning tool should distinguish generated interpretations from the original material.

Choose what to keep

Explicit permissions allow the learner to choose which sites are included in a study session. Pausing should immediately prevent new information from being accepted into the study record. Deleting a record should remove both the captured sections and practice questions derived from them.
